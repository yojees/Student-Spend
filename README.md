# 💰 StudentSpend

> A simple and modern expense tracking web app designed specifically for students.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://studentspendyojees.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 About

**StudentSpend** is a student-focused personal finance and expense tracking application that helps students understand where their money goes.

The app provides a clean and simple interface for recording expenses, managing budgets, setting financial goals, and viewing spending insights.

The goal of StudentSpend is to make money management **simple, visual, and easy to understand** for students.

---

## ✨ Features

- 💸 **Expense Tracking**
  - Quickly add and record daily expenses.
  - Organize spending by categories.

- 📊 **Spending Insights**
  - Understand where your money is going.
  - View spending patterns and summaries.

- 💰 **Budget Management**
  - Set monthly spending limits.
  - Keep track of your remaining budget.

- 🎯 **Financial Goals**
  - Create and manage personal savings goals.
  - Track your progress over time.

- 📈 **Analytics**
  - Visualize your spending habits.
  - Get a clearer picture of your financial activity.

- ⚡ **Simple User Experience**
  - Clean and modern interface.
  - Designed with students in mind.

- 📱 **Responsive Design**
  - Works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

---

## 🎯 Why StudentSpend?

Students often have limited budgets but many small daily expenses.

Food, transportation, subscriptions, entertainment, college supplies, and other purchases can quickly add up.

StudentSpend helps answer a simple question:

> **"Where is my money going?"**

By tracking expenses and understanding spending habits, students can make smarter financial decisions.
